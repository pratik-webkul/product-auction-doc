WhatsApp integration for the [Product Auction App](https://apps.shopify.com/product-auction) on Shopify offers a seamless way to keep your customers informed and engaged throughout the bidding process.

With real-time notifications, bid confirmations, and auction updates sent directly through WhatsApp, you can provide a more engaging and responsive experience for your users.

This seamless communication helps boost bidder participation and significantly improves the overall efficiency of your auction process.

This WhatsApp integration for Shopify auctions bridges the gap between your store and your customers by leveraging one of the world’s most widely used messaging platforms.

To use WhatsApp integration for auctions on your store, you’ll first need to enable the **‘WhatsApp Notification Integration’** feature within the **[Product Auction app](https://apps.shopify.com/product-auction)**.

![WhatsApp Integration for Shopify Auction Features](https://cdnblog.webkul.com/blog/wp-content/uploads/2025/06/whtasappftureapp-1200x857.webp)

Once activated, you’ll be prompted to approve a monthly charge of **$15 USD** to use this feature.

After enabling the feature app, if you want to send WhatsApp notifications to customers, you’ll also need to activate the **‘Send WhatsApp Notification to Customer’** configuration.

If you want to send WhatsApp notifications to the admin regarding their auction products and related updates, please ensure that the **'Send WhatsApp Notification to Admin'** configuration is enabled.

**Note:** When you enable the **'Send WhatsApp Notification to Admin'** configuration, you must enter the **admin’s WhatsApp number** in the correct format.

The number should include the **country code without the “+” sign**, and there must be a **hyphen (“-”)** between the country code and the phone number.

To do this, navigate to **Product Auction App** → **Configuration** → **Auction Config**, and enable the corresponding option.

![WhatsApp Integration for Shopify Auction Features](https://cdnblog.webkul.com/blog/wp-content/uploads/2025/06/confidwhatspp-1200x705.webp)

To use this feature, please add your WhatsApp credentials by navigating to the Product Auction App >> Configuration.

Click on the three dots in the right corner and select 'WhatsApp Credential' from the dropdown menu.

![WhatsApp Integration for Shopify Auction Features](https://cdnblog.webkul.com/blog/wp-content/uploads/2025/06/credtemp-1200x776.webp)

Now, as the store admin, you’ll need to fill in the following fields within the app to start using the **WhatsApp Notification** feature.

*   WHATSAPP BUSINESS ACCOUNT ID
*   PHONE NUMBER ID
*   PHONE NUMBER
*   ACCESS TOKEN

## How to Get a WhatsApp Access Token

#### We need to get a permanent access token to use in the WhatsApp Integration configuration

You can find your **temporary access token** that expires in 24 hours and your testing phone number in the WhatsApp product settings.

But in order to use the WhatsApp API, we need a **permanent access token.**

To generate a permanent token, go to **Business Manager Settings** and navigate to **Users > System Users**.

![WhatsApp Integration for Shopify Auction Features](https://cdnblog.webkul.com/blog/wp-content/uploads/2025/06/generatetoken-1200x533.webp)

Click on your username, then select **"Generate Token"**. Choose your app from the list and enable the necessary permissions, such as **`whatsapp_business_management`** and **`whatsapp_business_messaging`**.

![WhatsApp Integration for Shopify Auction Features](https://cdnblog.webkul.com/blog/wp-content/uploads/2025/06/selectapp-2-1200x643.webp)

Finally, click on _'Generate Token'_; doing so will provide you with your WhatsApp Access Token.

First, copy the generated access token, and then paste it into the Access Token field under the WhatsApp Credentials section.

![WhatsApp Integration for Shopify Auction Features](https://cdnblog.webkul.com/blog/wp-content/uploads/2025/06/tokencopy.webp)

**Please note:** Since this token is displayed only once, be sure to copy and save it immediately. After you leave the page, you won’t be able to retrieve it again.

## Callback URL and Verify Token

In order to send messages, you’ll first need to configure your Callback URL and then subscribe to the _'message'_ webhook.

You can locate the _Callback URL_ and _Verify Token_ fields in your Meta Dashboard under the WhatsApp section > Configuration. After verification, make sure to save your changes.

How to Create WhatsApp Notification Templates?
----------------------------------------------

We have provided default WhatsApp templates to help you get started quickly.

To set them up, simply open the **Product Auction App**, navigate to the **WhatsApp Create Templates** section, and follow the steps to configure your notification templates.

Then, navigate to the **Configuration** section, click on the **three-dot menu (⋮)**, and select **‘WhatsApp Create Templates’** from the dropdown options.

![WhatsApp Integration for Shopify Auction Features](https://cdnblog.webkul.com/blog/wp-content/uploads/2025/06/whattemp-1-1200x800.webp)

Once there, you'll see a list of events that can trigger WhatsApp notifications for bidders—such as Auction win, Purchase reminder, Cancel winner, etc.

For each event, you can click on the **‘Create Default Template’** button to generate the corresponding notification template.

After a template is successfully created, its status will update to **Created**. Events that haven't been configured yet will continue to display the status as **Pending**.

In addition to bidder notifications, you can also create default templates for **admin-related events**.

These templates ensure that admins receive relevant WhatsApp notifications about activities related to auctions on their products.

This setup helps ensure both bidders and admins stay informed throughout the auction process, enhancing transparency and engagement.

**Note:** When you create a WhatsApp notification template using the **‘Create Default Template’** button, there's no need to map the template manually—it will be automatically mapped for you.

## How to map the WhatsApp template in the Auction App

After importing the WhatsApp template into the Product Auction App, you need to map it within the app.

To do this, navigate to the **Configuration** section, click on the **three dots (⋮)**, and select **WhatsApp Import Templates**.

![WhatsApp Integration for Shopify Auction Features](https://cdnblog.webkul.com/blog/wp-content/uploads/2025/06/mapptemp-1200x695.webp)

You will see the imported template listed there.

Click on the **three-dot menu** under the **Action** column next to the template, then select the **‘Map Template’** option.

From there, choose the appropriate **Default Event** to map the template to. Once selected, click **Save** to complete the process.

![WhatsApp Integration for Shopify Auction Features](https://cdnblog.webkul.com/blog/wp-content/uploads/2025/06/temptwolay-1200x857.webp)

Once you map the template with the default event, you’ll also need to verify your **Callback URL** and **Verify Token** to ensure the integration works correctly.

## Frontend

Once you enable the 'Send WhatsApp Notification' configuration and a bidder places a bid, the system promptly displays a pop-up as soon as the bidder clicks the _'Place My Bid'_ button.

At this point, the pop-up will ask, 'Do you want to receive WhatsApp notifications?'

![receive](https://cdnblog.webkul.com/blog/wp-content/uploads/2025/06/notifiwha-1200x533.webp)

Once the bidder actively clicks "Yes," a new pop-up will immediately appear, prompting them to actively enter their country code and WhatsApp contact number.

Once they have entered the details, they must then click _'Yes'_ again in order to start receiving notifications on WhatsApp.

![number](https://cdnblog.webkul.com/blog/wp-content/uploads/2025/06/enternumb-1200x533.webp)

## WhatsApp Notification Logs

You can check the **WhatsApp notification history** in the **WhatsApp Notification Logs** section.

![WhatsApp Integration for Shopify Auction Features](https://cdnblog.webkul.com/blog/wp-content/uploads/2025/06/logs-1200x695.webp)

From there, you’ll be able to view the notifications triggered for bidders or admins, along with detailed information about each event.

The log displays key details such as **Notification ID**, **Auction ID**, **Customer Name**, **Event Name**, **Contact Number**, **Status**, and the **Sent Date**.

Additionally, when you click on the **Status** (e.g., _Sent_ or _Failed_), you can view more detailed insights.

If the status shows **Sent**, you can view whether the recipient has read the message or not, along with the **delivery time** and **read time**, including the exact **date and time**.

If the status shows **Failed**, clicking on the **info (i) button** displays the reason for the failure, helping you identify and resolve the issue.

![WhatsApp Integration for Shopify Auction Features](https://cdnblog.webkul.com/blog/wp-content/uploads/2025/06/failednotif-1200x625.webp)

In the **WhatsApp Notification Logs**, any WhatsApp notification sent or triggered to the admin will also appear in the notification history.

However, in the case of admin notifications, the **Auction ID** will **not be displayed** in the log.

## Warning: Template Edited

When you make any changes to a template on the **Meta** side, a warning labeled **"Template Edited"** will appear in the **WhatsApp Import Template** section.

![Whatsapp](https://cdnblog.webkul.com/blog/wp-content/uploads/2025/06/warntemp-1.webp)

The system displays this warning specifically for the template that you have edited. Clicking on the warning will open a pop-up with two options: **Ignore Warning** or **Map Template**.

![WhatsApp Integration for Shopify Auction Features](https://cdnblog.webkul.com/blog/wp-content/uploads/2025/06/waningwh.webp)

If you choose **Map Template**, please ensure that you **verify and update the mappings** to align correctly with the modified content and the appropriate event.

The system removes the warning once it successfully maps the template.

Alternatively, if you click on **Ignore Warning**, the system will dismiss the warning without applying any changes to the updated content.
